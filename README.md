## 🧠 What is Zyra?

> **What if building a website was as simple as describing it?**

**Zyra.ai** is an AI-powered website builder that turns natural-language prompts into websites.

No blank canvas.  
No staring at thousands of lines of code.  
No hours spent building boilerplate.

Just describe your idea and let Zyra do the heavy lifting.
